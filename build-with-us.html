Build with us

Some of the important places here are still empty.

FFRI-NM is still early enough that some of its future research programs, institutional roles and operating structures do not yet have people attached to them.

We are interested in independent researchers, builders, critics and institutional thinkers who may want to shape something while it is still genuinely shapeable.
Why now

Early means unfinished.

FFRI-NM is not presenting a finished institution and asking people to fit themselves into it. Its constitutional lineage exists, its first research project is public, and its first major constitutional proposal is under review — but much of the institution that may eventually surround those foundations has not yet been built.

That creates uncertainty. It also creates something that becomes harder to find later: room for consequential contribution.

We do not know how large or influential FFRI-NM will become. We will not pretend that we do. What we can do is preserve a public record of who actually helped build what.

What's open

What could be yours?

Contribution before position.

FFRI-NM cannot honestly promise where an early contribution will lead. It can distinguish between participation and authorship, and it can preserve provenance.

What could be yours?

Something that exists because you built it.

A research question.A method.A dataset.A criticism that changes the institution.A technical system.A future field of inquiry.

Not because FFRI-NM assigns historical importance in advance, but because verifiable work should remain attributable to the people who actually did it.

No hidden promise

Possibility is not a contract.

Participation does not imply employment, compensation, governance authority, funding, a future appointment or any guaranteed professional benefit. FFRI-NM does not offer roles or resources that do not yet exist.

Money, technical control, visibility or the size of a contribution do not by themselves create constitutional authority within FFRI-NM.

The upside is uncertain because the future is uncertain.

An early contribution may remain a small entry in the history of a small project. If FFRI-NM grows, the same contribution may acquire greater professional or historical significance. We do not promise either outcome.

Come with something real

You do not need our permission to think.

You do not need a polished application. If you see an unanswered question, a flaw, a research direction, a system you could build, or a role that should exist, tell us what you see and what you would do with it.

We would rather receive one concrete idea than a general statement of enthusiasm.
