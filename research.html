Independent Research · Published in association with FFRI-NM

When Experience Survives the Agent

Testing Causal Operational Inheritance Across Successor Replacement

Navi Musaget · Methodological position paper with a controlled proof-of-concept · Published 25 September 2026 · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22968201

Introduces causal operational inheritance (COI) and the six-part COI-6 assay for testing whether an experience-produced operational mediator remains causally operative across predecessor–successor replacement. The controlled proof-of-concept reports a narrow positive result for an engineered mediator and preserves a failed test of less-prestructured significance selection as a boundary on the claim.

Project 01 · Research Note 01

When AI Becomes Evidence

A Comparative Audit of Machine-Signal Disclosure in University Academic Integrity Procedures

Version 1.0 · Published 21 September 2026 · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22879568

A comparative audit of public academic-integrity procedures at 24 purposively selected universities, examining how AI-detector outputs move from technical signals into institutional evidence, disclosure and contestability. The sample is exploratory and purposive; the study does not estimate prevalence, rank universities or test detector accuracy.

How this index works

Association does not erase provenance.

FFRI-NM distinguishes between research conducted as an FFRI-NM project and independent research published in association with the foundation. The label on each record states which relationship applies.

Research pages are records, not endorsements of stronger claims.

Each work should be read within the scope, limitations and authorship stated in its own publication record. New work will be added here rather than accumulated on the homepage.
