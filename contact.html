Contact FFRI-NM

Come with something real.

A question, a criticism, a research direction, something you could build, or a role you think should exist. You do not need a polished application. You do need something concrete enough for us to understand what you see.

What are you bringing?

There is one public contact channel, but not every conversation starts in the same place. These prompts are here to make the first message useful for both sides.

Research

I have a research idea.

A question, study, dataset, replication or extension that could fit FFRI-NM's field of inquiry.

Tell us: What is the question? Why does it matter? What would you actually do next?

Start this message →
Challenge

I found a problem or disagreement.

A weakness in our research, reasoning, governance design, source base or institutional assumptions.

Tell us: What do you think is wrong? What is the strongest evidence, source or argument for your view?

Challenge us →
Build

I could build something.

A method, dataset, technical system, research tool, process or other concrete piece of infrastructure.

Tell us: What is missing? What could you build? What would you need from FFRI-NM to test the idea?

Role

I see a role I could fill.

A real function you believe FFRI-NM needs — including an emerging research, advisory, governance or operating role.

Tell us: What function is missing? Why does it matter now? What would you take responsibility for?

Write to us

One channel, actively monitored.

You can also write directly without choosing a category. Written correspondence is currently monitored directly by founder Navi Musaget.

Current public contact
navimusaget@gmail.com
Please include FFRI-NM in the subject line. If your message concerns a specific project, include its name or number as well.
Concrete messages are easier to act on.

We would rather receive one specific question, objection or proposal than a general statement of enthusiasm. FFRI-NM is in an early phase and cannot promise an individual response to every message.

A message creates no appointment, employment relationship, funding commitment or governance authority. If a conversation develops into something more formal, that would require a separate explicit process.
